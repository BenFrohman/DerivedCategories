# Two caveats, not one finding

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman).
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Apache-2.0.

This note exists so the two facts in docs/FT_PROGRAMS.md are not glued
into a new theorem called "strange 3-variable heterotic c=9."

They are independent non-applications of *existing* theorems to this F.
They are not a discovery. They do not deserve a Clay or BH preprint as
a new result. They do deserve a one-page teaching split.

## Fact 1. Strange duality is three-variable

Ebeling–Takahashi (arXiv:1003.1590): for an invertible polynomial f(x,y,z)
in *three* variables, Dolgachev numbers of (f, G_f) equal Gabrielov numbers
of f^T, and conversely.

Our F is a sum of three chain blocks in *six* variables. The 3-variable
theorem does not name Dolgachev/Gabrielov numbers for V(F).

Ebeling–Gusein-Zade monodromy-zeta duality (arXiv:1008.4021) *does*
apply to chain type in any number of variables. That is a zeta identity,
not strange duality of Dolgachev numbers, and not heterotic strings.

## Fact 2. Heterotic c=9 is a threefold

Gepner / heterotic compactification to 4d spacetime uses a Calabi–Yau
*threefold*: N=2 central charge c = 3 * 3 = 9.

Our X = V(F) is a Calabi–Yau *fourfold*: LG central charge ĉ = 4,
N=2 c = 12. Different dimension, different charge, different compactification.

The Fermat sextic Gepner point is (k=4)^6 on x0^6+···+x5^6, which is
not this chain-sum F.
