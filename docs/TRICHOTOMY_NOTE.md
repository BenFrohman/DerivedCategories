# Blowup warning and Hodge trichotomy

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Apache-2.0.

## Bl_Π X is not Y

Blowing up a surface in a fourfold adds the exceptional divisor E with

    K_{Bl_Π X} = π* K_X + 2E = 2E ≄ O.

The blowup is not Calabi–Yau and is not a Fourier–Mukai partner of X.
That sentence is a warning, not a construction.

## Bl_Π X is not a Hodge disproof

Hodge on X asks whether every class in H^{2,2}(X) ∩ H^4(X,Q) is a Q-combination of surfaces on X.
[Π] and [S] = h² − [Π] are such surfaces. Those two classes are instances of the conjecture, not witnesses against it.
Classes on Bl_Π X do not disprove Hodge on X.

## Trichotomy

- Hodge (what Clay pays to prove): ∀X ∀γ ∃ Z_i.
- Counterexample (what Clay pays to disprove): ∃X ∃γ with no such Z_i.
- Missing Lean instance: no uniform construct. That is neither of the first two.

An extra-class fourfold with an explicit surface is the easy arrow.
