## 1. Ideal of L in P^5

$$
I(L_{a,b,c})
=
\Bigl\langle
b x_3-a x_4,\;
c x_3-a x_5,\;
c x_4-b x_5
\Bigr\rangle
\subset\mathbb{C}[x_0,\ldots,x_5].
$$

These are the 2×2 minors of

$$
\begin{pmatrix} x_3 & x_4 & x_5 \\ a & b & c \end{pmatrix}.
$$

## 2. Discriminant in the base P^2

$$
\mu = a^6+b^6+c^6.
$$

On V(μ), with abc ≠ 0, R is independent of t: a cone on the plane quintic, singular at [0:0:0:1]. The theorem's smoothness hypothesis is exactly the complement of V(abc μ).

## 3. Hodge numbers of a smooth fibre S

Smooth quintic surface in P^3:

$$
K_S\simeq\mathcal{O}_S(1),\qquad K_S^2=5,\qquad e(S)=c_2(S)=55,
\qquad
\chi(\mathcal{O}_S)=\frac{K_S^2+e(S)}{12}=5.
$$

So p_g=4, q=0. Classical, not special to this F.
