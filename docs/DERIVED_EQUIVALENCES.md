# Derived equivalences (applied to this host)

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Apache-2.0.
Status: Literature note plus one application. Not a partner theorem.

## Definition

An exact equivalence Φ : D^b(A) → D^b(B) of bounded derived categories of coherent sheaves.
On smooth projective varieties over ℂ, Orlov: Φ is Fourier–Mukai for a kernel E ∈ D^b(A×B).

Necessary numerical conditions: dim A = dim B (Serre / Hochschild dimension), compatible HH / Hodge of HH.

## Reconstruction

Bondal–Orlov: if K or K^{-1} is ample, D^b reconstructs the variety.
Kawamata: D-equivalence vs K-equivalence is conjectural in general; proven in many low-dimensional cases.

Worked families: K3 Mukai–Orlov; abelian Poincaré; cubic Kuznetsov component A_X (not the whole D^b of a Fano cubic); flops when K-equivalent.

## This host

- D^b(X) is a 4-CY category. Serre functor [4]. Reconstruction fails because K_X ≃ O.
- The existing equivalence is Orlov D^b(X) ≃ HMF^{gr}(F).
- S is a surface of general type, K_S ≃ O_S(1) ample. Bondal–Orlov reconstructs S.
- D^b(S) has Serre functor (− ⊗ K_S)[2]. So D^b(X) ≣ D^b(S).
- Therefore S is not a Fourier–Mukai partner of X. Do not set Y := S.

A fourfold partner Y ≢ X remains an open conjecture.
