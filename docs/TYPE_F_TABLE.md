The real geometric variable is the variety

    V := V(F) ⊂ P^5.

F is the equation. V is the host. Y and E are named slots on this table.
They are not filled.

## Names

    F = x0^5 x3 + x3^6 + x1^5 x4 + x4^6 + x2^5 x5 + x5^6
    V = V(F)
    Π = V(x3, x4, x5) ⊂ V
    Y = unwritten fourfold, not isomorphic to V
    E = unwritten kernel in D^b(V × Y)

Do not rename the polynomial to V. That letter is already the vanishing locus.

## Y and E as they stand

Y is the second fourfold in the open partner conjecture for this V.
E is a kernel on the product V × Y.
Neither has generators. Listing them does not inhabit them.
O_Δ is not E. S is not Y. X_A (double EPW) is not Y: Hodge numbers fail.
