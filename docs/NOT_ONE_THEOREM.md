## 1. Strange duality is three-variable

Ebeling–Takahashi: Dolgachev ↔ Gabrielov numbers for invertible
weighted-homogeneous polynomials in *three* variables (surface
singularities). That is existing literature.

This F has *six* variables. The 3-variable theorem does not apply to V(F).
One block u^5 v + v^6 is a plane-curve singularity (μ=30). Adding a dummy
Fermat factor to force three variables is a different singularity.

## 2. Heterotic c=9 is threefold physics

Heterotic compactification to 4d uses a Calabi–Yau *threefold*,
central charge c=9 (or ĉ=3). This X=V(F) is a Calabi–Yau *fourfold*,
ĉ(F)=4. Different dimension, different charge, different compactification.

Gepner (k=4)^6 is the Fermat sextic, not this chain-sum F.

## What is not claimed

There is no new theorem “strange duality + heterotic = Hodge miss.”
There is no preprint-worthy discovery in the glue. The two facts are
reasons *not* to quote those theorems as applying to this fourfold.
