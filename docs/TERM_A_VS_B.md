# Term A versus Term B

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman).
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Apache-2.0.

Official space: Hdg^2(X) = H^4(X,Q) ∩ H^{2,2}(X). Not P^4.

    A: ∀X ∀γ ∈ Hdg^2(X), γ ∈ im(cl_X)
    B: ∃X ∃γ ∈ Hdg^2(X), γ ∉ im(cl_X)

They are a statement and its negation. Exactly one is true.
That does not mean a missing term of B is a proof of A.

If a geometric term of B is supplied, A is false.
If a term of A is supplied, B is false.
Neither term is supplied here. The conjecture is open.
